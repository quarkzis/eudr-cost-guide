EUDR Due Diligence Cost Guide

Pilot release – October 2026

This guide explains how the EUDR Due Diligence Costs Calculator estimates the budget for setting up and running a due diligence system under the EU Deforestation Regulation.

Cost types

  • Set-up costs: one-off effort to establish the system.
  • On-going costs: recurring yearly effort to operate it.
